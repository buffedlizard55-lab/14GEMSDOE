Round 6 — five new geological hypotheses distinct from R1–R5

Real competition rasters, hide-and-recover component folds, emission calibrated on hidden CALIB traces only, official DTI metric. Top candidate R6-1 horsetail splay fan beats baseline on sparse AND far in 4/4 folds — first arm in this project to meet the pre-registered promote rule on real data. No submission slot is spent in this session; the validated field is built and format-gated for next slot.

Metric reminder: DTI = T/(0.2T+0.2E+0.8·N). Adding a pixel is worth it iff ΔT/ΔE > 0.2·D/(1-0.2·D) = 0.0323 at D=0.1563. Scaling a support up never lowers score. Binary 1.0 emission optimal given support. Emission budget calibrated on CALIB, not TEST.

R6-1Horsetail splay fan at fault terminations. Catalogue geometry (training_labels.tif) + det_elev (band 12) + det_elev_slope (band 19) + structure tensor of detrended elevation. At each endpoint, 120° fan (60° each side of outward strike) radius 0.5–2 km (5–20 px), weight = (1-d/R)·exp(-Δθ²/2σ²), σ=35°, modulated by slope magnitude (≥0.3 even where slope low). Max over all tips, off-trace only, Gaussian σ=1 px.Angular divergence of short lineaments radiating from trace endpoints within 0.5–2 km — horsetail splay.Faulds et al. 2026: "some FSS (e.g., fault tips) are characterized by minor faults with minimal surface ruptures, making them difficult to recognize even with high-resolution lidar" (KB S14). Catalogue records main strand but not distributed splay. Terminations 22% of systems (2012), horsetails 20% later vintage (KB S2). Exactly the "small connecting structures" brief hypothesizes are omitted.R3A = decaying cones past single tips (isotropic wedge, no fan, no slope modulation). R5-1 = relay interior between overlapping strands, not tip splay. R3D = curvature-linkage point-wise, no tip-anchored fan. No sibling arm targets horsetail fans.low
R6-2Normal × strike-slip intersection halos. Catalogue + geod_shearrate (band 7) + geod_dilaterate (band 8) + tmi_hg (band 3) + junction density. (1) Junction pixels with ≥3 neighbours where strike variance >45° (orthogonal). (2) Near-miss tip pairs within 2 km whose strikes differ >45°. Halo = Gaussian σ=10 px (1 km) around candidates, weight = shear_norm × dilation_norm × (1-parallelism) × junction_density_norm.Intersection density of catalogue traces where strike difference >45° + geodetic shear high + dilation high, emitting 1–3 km halo.Intersections host 22% of systems (Faulds 2012) and 26.1% of FSS (2026), but small linking faults at intersection buried under basin fill (Faulds: "basin-fill sediments obscure subsurface architecture"). Catalogue records crossing strands but not linking faults. Geodetic strain shows active deformation where no scarp.junction_density counts any junction, no strike filter, no halo, no geodetic weighting. relay_corridors requires sub-parallel (≤40°), opposite of this (≥45°). No arm uses shear × dilation product. Sibling H11–H41 have no orthogonal intersection detector.low
R6-3Conductive-base step / clay-cap edge. cond_surf (band 17) + depth_to_base_surf (band 15) + tmi (band 14) + iso_grav_anom_hg (band 18). HGM of depth_to_base: |∇ depth| via Sobel, 90th percentile ridge = fault-controlled base step. High cond ≥80th = clay cap. Magnetic low ≤20th = altered rocks. Coincidence: HGM ridge within 500 m of cond high AND mag low. Cap margin distance = distance transform + Gaussian decay σ=5 px.Horizontal gradient magnitude ridge of conductive base depth coincident with high conductivity and magnetic low — edge of geothermal clay cap.Faulds et al. 2026: "magnetic lows and low resistivity anomalies may respectively indicate altered rocks and clay caps at depth induced by geothermal activity" (KB S12). Fault feeding cap not necessarily mapped, cap invisible to scarp-based compilation. 39% of Great Basin systems are blind (KB S3).N3 = (high cond AND mag low) mask → margin, but no depth_to_base gradient, and N3 killed on synthetic not real. R5-3 = tilt angle, no conductivity. No arm uses depth_to_base HGM. Sibling H18 used tc band as tilt, not conductivity.medium
R6-4Slip & dilation tendency corridors (external INGENIOUS release). External: Shapefile_INGENIOUS area.zip from DOI 10.5066/P9YL58W6 (Siler 2022, USGS) → rasterized slip_tendency + dilation_tendency (0–1) on competition grid EPSG:32611 100 m bilinear. Transform: rasterize shapefile, interpolate between segments via IDW, emit where dilation>0.7 AND slip>0.5 AND catalogue absent within 300 m. Weight = dilation × slip × (1-catalogue_proximity).Slip tendency = τ/σn, dilation tendency = (σ1-σn)/(σ1-σ3) — high where fault optimally oriented for slip/opening under ambient stress.Catalogue compilation geometry-only, not mechanics-based. Faults optimally oriented for dilation more likely permeable and host geothermal fluid (Siler et al., INGENIOUS). Such faults may be subtle/buried but mechanically favored, so missing from Qfaults. INGENIOUS authors calculated this to "help identify faults likely to host as-yet-undiscovered hydrothermal processes" (ScienceBase abstract, verified 2026-09-29).No arm in this repo or siblings uses external stress-derived rasters. Prompt explicitly lists "Slip and dilation tendency from the INGENIOUS release" as feature — never implemented until now. Sibling H41 uses distance-to-catalogue as anti-target, not mechanics.med-high
R6-5Paleo-lake shoreline suppression + sub-lake fault enhancement. det_elev (band 12) + det_elev_slope (band 19) + iso_grav_anom (band 13) + iso_grav_anom_hg (band 18) + structure tensor. Detect shorelines: high coherence >0.7 + low slope <30th percentile + sub-horizontal strike. Two outputs: shoreline_mask (suppress FP where scarps are shorelines) and sub_lake_enhanced: det_elev < shoreline elevation AND gravity HGM high (basin structure beneath lake sediments). Weight = gravity_HGM_norm × (1-shoreline_proximity).Detect paleo-shoreline terraces as high-coherence low-curvature lineaments; sub-lake fault enhancement where gravity indicates basin structure beneath former lake bottom.Faulds et al. 2026: "much of the GBR was inundated by late Pleistocene lakes, and thus faults that have not ruptured in the Holocene are obscured by lake sediments and shoreline features" (KB S14). Hermant et al. 2025: CNN detects paleo-shorelines as false positives (KB M4) — shoreline scarps look like fault scarps. Faults under lake beds systematically missing.N4 idea (lidar scarp + shoreline suppression) pre-registered but never implemented due to missing external paleo-shoreline dataset (FLAG #1b). This arm derives shorelines from det_elev + slope + coherence, and uses gravity to see through lake fill (Faulds: gravity gradients defined terminations in basins). R5-4 = profile curvature alone, no shoreline concept. Sibling H32 uses external 10 m DEM, not detrended elevation.medium

Gate protocol (same as R5, pre-registered)

  1. Truth: official catalogue 3,199 components.
  2. Split per fold: TEST 20% / CALIB 20% / HIDE 35% / visible 25%. Training view excludes TEST and HIDE; prediction view = full catalogue minus TEST (submission-time context). Anti-leak by construction.
  3. Calibration: emission policy (threshold / top-k) chosen on hidden CALIB only.
  4. Learner: weighted logistic regression 150 GD iters lr 0.5 L2 1e-3, 20k pos + 40k neg sampled from visible, standardized by training mean/std. Identical for every arm.
  5. Truth protocols: dense (all TEST), sparse (20% subsample), far (TEST >1 km from any context trace).
  6. Decision rule: promote only if beats baseline on both sparse and far in ≥3 of 4 folds. No slot spent until met.
