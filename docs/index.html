Find the faults nobody has mapped yet.

The GEMS Prize (US DOE Office of Geothermal) pays for algorithms that surface geological faults missing from the public USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue — faults that indicate geothermal resources in the GeoDAWN region of Nevada and eastern California. Score: a distance-weighted Tversky index that punishes missed faults four times harder than false alarms. Competition closes Dec 3, 2026 23:59 UTC; prize pool $300,000 (verified on the official page — see Data & sources).

Why the team kept scoring 0.1563 — and what changes now

Verified autopsy (2026-09-28). The prior project sites GEMSDOE1 and 5GEMSDOE ship byte-identical prediction fields — the same artifact sha256 7f00890a…, same payload 259,495 runs, same value histogram. Identical pixels score identical numbers, which is why GEMSDOE1, 5GEMSDOE, 8GEMSDOE and GEMSDOE2 all landed on 0.1563 / 0.1560 and why three unrelated leaderboard accounts (extradr19, SDCF9, smashi34) currently tie at exactly 0.1563. The score is not a mystery floor — it is the score of that one field. Every future run in this repo gets a unique name, its own content hash, and its own note (scripts/build_submission.py enforces it), and no idea reaches a submission slot without first beating the current best on the spatially-blocked holdout. Details: Strategy.

The five moves we are making (distinct ideas, not the same idea re-dressed)

Move 1 · structural completion
relay ramps & tips
emit the small connecting faults the catalogue omits at step-overs, horsetails and intersections (Faulds et al.: 32% / 22% / 22% / 8% of systems)
Move 2 · hydrothermal expression
heat · springs · sinter
faults feed hot springs and sinter deposits; where expression exists without a catalogue fault, an unmapped structure is the explanation
Move 3 · magnetic lineaments
edge transforms
structure-tensor / edge detection on the GeoDAWN magnetics for concealed faults with no surface scarp
Move 4 · completeness residuals
strain vs. catalogue
where strain rate and relief predict deformation the catalogue does not show, flag the gap
Move 5 · hide-and-recover
no leaky training
the model only ever learns to recover hidden traces; “distance to known fault = 0” is never the answer

Each move names its layers, its physical signature, and its evidence on the Hypotheses page, ranked by expected DTI gain and implementation cost, with the free official data source that backs it.

What the score actually rewards

False negatives
β = 0.8
missing a hidden fault costs 4× an equal false positive
False positives
α = 0.2
exploratory confidence on genuinely plausible faults is cheap
Match tolerance
300 m triangular
a trace within 100 m of the hidden fault still earns 0.67 of full credit
Phase 2 upside
$250,000
experts verify your novel faults and add them to the labels — genuine discoveries are re-scored as truth
